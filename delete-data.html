Deleting your data

Texas Hold'em by FergTech. You can delete your profile and its data at any time.

In the app (quickest)

  1. Open the app and tap With Friends.
  2. Tap Profile & privacy.
  3. Tap Delete my profile and confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Deleted straight away: your profile (name, profile ID, key hash, friend code), your friends list and invites.
  • Kept without your name: league results show "Deleted player" so other members' tables still add up.
  • Deleted automatically: tables you played at are removed 2 days after their last use; abuse-protection counters after a day.
  • On your phone: the app's saved data (profile key, stats, leagues list) is cleared.

Can't open the app any more?

If you've lost your phone, or no longer have the app, ask us to delete your data by opening a deletion request with your player name and friend code (if you know it). We'll confirm within 30 days.

More detail is in the privacy policy.
