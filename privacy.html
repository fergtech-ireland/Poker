Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026. Texas Hold'em is made by FergTech ("we"). This policy explains what the app stores and why.

Playing against the computer

Everything stays on your phone. Your wins, losses and win percentage are kept in your browser's storage and are never sent to us.

Playing with friends

To play with friends we store, on our server (Supabase, hosted in the EU, Ireland):

  • Your profile: the player name you choose, a random profile ID, a one-way scrambled copy (hash) of a secret key kept on your phone, your friend code, your friends list, pending table invites, and which leagues you're in.
  • Tables you play at: player names, chips, cards dealt and the moves made. Tables are deleted 2 days after they were last used.
  • League tables: your name and results (points, wins, hands won) in leagues you join.
  • Abuse protection: a scrambled form of your IP address with a request count, kept for up to a day, to stop spam.

We don't ask for your email, phone number, location or contacts. We don't show ads, don't sell or share your data, and don't use tracking or analytics.

Other players at your tables and in your leagues can see your player name, your chips, your moves and, at showdown, your cards. Friends can see your name and friend code.

Why we use it

Only to run the game: dealing cards, keeping tables in sync between phones, showing friends lists, invites and league tables, and protecting the service from abuse. The legal basis is providing the service you asked for (contract) and our legitimate interest in keeping it secure.

Deleting your data

In the app, open With Friends → Profile & privacy → Delete my profile. This deletes your profile and friends list from our server straight away, replaces your name in league tables with "Deleted player", and clears the app's data on that phone. See Deleting your data for other options.

Your rights

Under GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how it's used. Contact us through the FergTech GitHub page linked below. You can also complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

Children

The game simulates gambling (no real money) and is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change, and significant changes will be shown in the app.
